Download a whole playlist

Paste a playlist link and every video in it goes into the list. Video Download downloads several at once and tries again by itself if the connection drops.

Steps

  1. Copy the playlist link and click Paste. All its videos are added to the list.
  2. Choose the format, for example Video – best quality (MP4) or Audio only – MP3. It applies to everything waiting in the list; a single row can get its own format with its format button.
  3. Click Download.

Good to know

  • A link to one video that belongs to a playlist downloads only that video. For the whole list, turn on Downloads → Video link from a playlist: download the whole playlist.
  • Downloads → Simultaneous downloads: from 1 to 4 downloads at the same time.
  • If the connection drops, the download is tried again up to 3 times.

Video Download is free for Windows and Mac.
