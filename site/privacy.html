Privacy

Neither the website nor the app collects data about you.

This website

  • The website has no cookies, analytics, ads or forms and stores nothing in your browser.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any server, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address for security and operation; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The installer is downloaded from GitHub (same terms as above).
  • The support button leads to PayPal; PayPal only receives data when you open it, under its own rules.
  • Problem reports go to GitHub Issues and are public (a GitHub account is needed). The app's problem report shortens links to the site name and hides your Windows user name; don't add passwords, cookies or personal data.
  • Contributions are processed by PayPal. As the recipient, the author sees what PayPal shows for every payment (e.g. the payer's name and e-mail) and uses it only for bookkeeping, never for advertising or anything else.
  • The Share button uses your device's own sharing or opens the network you choose; that network gets the link only when you click it.
  • Messages to abnpsdev@gmail.com (the project's contact address, a Gmail mailbox at Google) are used only to reply to you and are never passed on or used for anything else.

The app

In short: the author of the program does not collect any data about you. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.

1. What the program does not do

The program does not send the author any data, usage statistics or error reports. There are no ads and no analytics.

2. Who the program connects to

  • The site you download from (video, audio, thumbnails, subtitles).
  • GitHub (api.github.com, github.com): checking for and downloading a new version of the program, at most once a day and when you click "Check for updates".
  • PyPI (pypi.org, files.pythonhosted.org): checking for and downloading the site reader yt-dlp, at most once a day.
  • PayPal only if you click “Support the project”: the PayPal contribution page opens in your browser, and the program sends nothing.

These services can see your IP address and the program version; their own privacy policies apply.

3. What is stored on your device

On Windows: settings (registry: HKCU\Software\VideoDownload), the queue and history (links, titles, paths, sizes, times), downloaded yt-dlp in %LOCALAPPDATA%\VideoDownload, and a local key for the browser connection. On Mac: app data in ~/Library/Application Support/VideoDownload and system app settings. On Android: settings, the queue (including links), history, diagnostics and downloaded yt-dlp in the app's private storage; completed files go to Downloads/Video Download or the Gallery/Music folders you choose. This data is not sent to the author.

4. Login cookies

Only if you allow it in the browser. Only cookies of the site you download from are passed to the program, they are kept only in memory, and the temporary file for yt-dlp is deleted immediately. They are never written to the queue, the history or reports. On Android: if you sign in to Instagram or TikTok in the app yourself (Settings → Instagram account / TikTok account), you enter your password only on that site's page; the sign-in cookies stay in the app's private storage, are given to yt-dlp for each read and download through a temporary file that is deleted right away, and are sent only to the site you signed in to. Signing out deletes them.

5. Browser extension

Permissions: watching network requests (to recognise video streams), a script on the page (to find the playing video and recognise DRM), the right-click menu, a local connection to the program (native messaging) and temporary per-tab storage; cookies only if you allow them. The extension sends nothing to the internet: it passes data only to the program on your computer, and only when you click.

6. Problem report

On desktop, a report is created only when you save it. On Android, a limited log in private app storage records event types, domains and HTTP status codes; “Copy report” copies these details to the clipboard. Android does not log URL paths and parameters, page contents or headers. You send a report only if you choose to.

7. Deleting data

On Windows, clear history in Downloads → Download history → Clear the list. Uninstalling removes the program, browser connection and downloaded yt-dlp; the queue, history and settings remain until you delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\VideoDownload and HKCU\Software\VideoDownload. On Mac, remove ~/Library/Application Support/VideoDownload and the app's settings to clear local data. On Android, uninstalling deletes private app storage (queue, history and log), while completed files in Downloads, Gallery or Music remain until you delete them separately.

8. Contact

As the author collects no data, there is no data they could provide, correct or delete. Questions: https://github.com/abnps/video-download/issues
